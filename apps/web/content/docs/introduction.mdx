Snappin is an AI-powered tool that automatically finds, extracts, and organizes invoices and receipts, so you don’t have to organize them manually.

## Prerequisites
Before you begin, make sure you have at least one of the following:

1. An email account.
2. A Google account.
2. A Microsoft account.

## Register
1. Navigate to the [registration](/signup) page.
2. Create your account.
3. Connect your Gmail or Outlook accounts to start automatic invoice extraction
