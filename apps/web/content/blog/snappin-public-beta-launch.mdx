After ten months of development, I am proud to announce that Snappin has officially launched in public beta.

This beta launch is an important milestone, but it’s just the starting point. The core engine is live and working, and now the focus shifts to refining accuracy, improving reliability, and expanding integrations based on real-world usage.

## What You Can Do Today
- Automatically extract invoices from emails
- Send receipts through whatsapp
- Capture key fields like vendor, date, amount, and totals
- Store and manage documents in one place
- Reduce manual data entry significantly

## Upcoming Features

- Bank statements conciliations
- Web portal extraction
- Retroactive
- IMAP email accounts
- Quickbook, dropbox, one drive integrations
- Custom reports

Snappin is still a little rough around the edges in some areas, but I'm working day and night to ship regular updates.

Please feel free to send me as much feedback as you like. My inbox is always open.

This is just the beginning.
