Outlook integration is now live.

You can connect your inbox and let Snappin automatically extract receipts, capture key details, and organize everything without manual work.

Setup takes less than a minute.

## How to connect your Outlook

1. Go to your Snappin [dashboard](/dashboard)
2. Click on **Add Account**
3. Click on **Connect** right to Outlook
4. Grant access permissions
5. You're all set

## Why this is useful

- Find hidden receipts in seconds
- All your receipts are structured and ready to use
- Export data as CSV or Excel
- Save hours of repetitive work every week

From there, receipts are processed automatically in the background.

This is a big step toward fully automated bookkeeping.

More improvements and integrations are on the way.
